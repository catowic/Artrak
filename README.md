ARTRAK

A small house.
The door isn’t locked.
But something inside is waiting for you.

A single-player, narrative-driven, decision-focused horror game.
Every choice carries weight.
Some doors never close again.

Features
• 17 rooms, hidden passages, and locked secrets
• 5 different endings (each more disturbing than the last)
• Item interaction and irreversible moments
• A slow-burn, unsettling atmosphere
• Built to make you say, “One more run—this time I’ll act differently”
• Turkish language, short but intense experience (15–40 min per ending)

Warning
Playing after 2:00 AM is at your own risk.

You can’t leave without answering these questions:

• Do you really want to escape?
• Or do you just want to see how far you can go?
• And what if it’s watching you?

After your first ending, don’t send the link to your friends.
At least wait until you’ve seen the second ending.
